Research Areas

Exploring the Aerospace Frontier.

RAVIE's research interests focus on technologies capable of shaping the future of flight and aerospace systems.

UAV / UAS

Exploring unmanned aerial systems, autonomous platforms, mission applications, and advanced aerial capabilities.

Rotorcraft

Studying rotorcraft technologies, aerodynamics, flight concepts, and emerging approaches to vertical flight.

Aircraft Safety

Investigating aircraft safety, risk, human factors, system reliability, and advanced safety concepts.

Aerospace Systems

Investigating integrated aerospace systems, avionics, control systems, structures, propulsion, and future architectures.

Future Technologies

Exploring next-generation materials, propulsion, autonomy, AI, and breakthrough aerospace technologies.
